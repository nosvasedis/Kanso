# Kanso badge design

Manrope is Kanso's default typeface. Ordinary labels use Semibold (600), open counters and restrained spacing. The font is bundled under SIL OFL 1.1. Brand lettering remains original artwork rather than being reset in Manrope. The complete custom glyph direction was developed with the built-in imagegen tool; the generated 25-motif direction board is saved as `badges/design/glyph-concept.png`. Production vectors in `assets/glyphs/` interpret those motifs with one 2.15-unit stroke, rounded ends and joins, and a shared 34-unit canvas. The production renderer and full prompt are retained in the workspace.

Outline is the default appearance. Four skins share typography and optical spacing. PNGs have a transparent 64px canvas, 8px horizontal padding and a maximum width of 352px. Most horizontal artwork uses a 46px optical height; stacked source captions and subtitles use more of the canvas for legibility. Nuvio draws the category border; PNGs contain no extra frame. No client font installation is required for image badges. The Sync Helper also uses bundled Manrope.

Generic badges combine meaningful Kanso glyphs with Manrope. Source captions sit below their icons: WEB-DL uses cloud/download, WEBRip screen/capture, and REMUX converging film tracks. Blu-ray retains the exact official disc/B emblem above a Manrope BLU-RAY caption. Numbered tiers put the source icon and a plain T1–T8 label on one upper row with a quiet separator, above the original-size source caption. Channels have a speaker before the number. Spoken languages use exact flags clipped into speech bubbles, with a horizontal language code. Subtitles use flag-filled folded documents with caption lines, beside a language code stacked above SUB, with no background panel behind the letters. This document silhouette and vertical lettering distinguish subtitles from spoken audio. Small captions and tier labels use Manrope Bold (700). The generated direction boards and prompts are retained in `badges/design/`, including `compact-sources-subtitles-concept.png` and `compact-sources-subtitles-prompt.txt`. Production uses 38 licensed flag vectors to preserve authentic stripes and emblems. `assets/spoken-flags/` and `assets/subtitle-flags/` contain the portable SVGs; `assets/flag-sources/` contains the MIT license, originals and source hashes. Compound picture/audio artwork uses the same quiet vertical separator as source tiers. Resolution badges use a framed display with the resolution inside and the quality label underneath, inspired by the supplied references: 4K/ULTRA HD, 1440p/QUAD HD, 1080p/FULL HD, 720p/HD and lower resolutions/SD. Outline and Transparent use hollow displays with colored and white ink respectively. Solid and Mono use filled display faces with reversed resolution lettering. The display frame belongs to the icon; Nuvio still draws the outer chip border. These are custom Kanso labels, not official certification marks. Cuts, restoration, picture and generic audio labels have matching semantic glyphs.

Outline and Transparent have different artwork. Outline glyph and lettering pixels match the border color; Transparent uses white ink. Both spoken and subtitle flags retain real colors in every skin. Special editions retain distinct individual hues. Source families and their tiers share consistent colors: blue WEB-DL/Blu-ray, cyan WEBRip/HDTV, lavender REMUX, muted blue HDRip, sand DVDRip and coral capture sources. Picture uses gold, audio/channels cyan, spoken language orchid and subtitles violet. Brand and special-edition hues are lightened when necessary to reach at least 5:1 contrast on the reference #151A21 surface. Resolution retains mint/amber/coral grouping. Solid selects black or white lettering by actual fill contrast, reaching at least 4.5:1. Contrast results apply to the checked reference surface; actual Nuvio themes can differ.

Three reported channel layouts were added: **1.0, 2.1 and 8.1**. Reported 8.1 no longer produces 7.1. Track channel values match exactly; file-level channel arrays choose one layout by precedence. Existing atom/tier/language/subtitle marker IDs are preserved and tested against a saved baseline. The preset-version marker changed, so the new formatter and packs must be updated together. Old formatter output cannot activate the new badges.

Logo artwork covers Dolby Vision/Atmos/TrueHD/Digital/Digital Plus, DTS/DTS:X/Master Audio, IMAX/Enhanced, all nine streaming services, Criterion, Blu-ray, HDR10+, FLAC and Opus. Generic technical concepts do not have a vendor mark invented for them. Combined formats pair a sourced logo with Manrope suffixes; these are Kanso compositions, not claimed official combined lockups.

`badges/design/manifest.json` records each badge's kind, glyph, source links and image hashes. `assets/badge-sources/sources.json` records 26 downloaded assets with byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Sources include brand-owned pages, official partner-distributed assets and Wikimedia reproductions of the brand marks, identified by their actual URLs. `research.json` records responses from brand-owned sites, including blocked pages. IMAX Enhanced retains the existing recognizable mark; its inherited image URL and brand reference are recorded. The previous appearance is archived in `badges/design/original-appearance.json`. Source type is not confused with a claim that every file came directly from a vendor download portal.

The 48 detected language identities stay exact internally; 38 display families appear in each language row. Spanish/Latino share ES; Portuguese regional variants share PT. Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati and Punjabi share IN, meaning **Indian languages**, for both spoken audio and subtitles. These are distinct languages, grouped only in presentation at the user's request. IN does not identify the exact language; the player's audio/subtitle picker still does. Preferred-track selection and subtitle filtering operate on the original language identities: Hindi preference cannot be satisfied by a Tamil-only track. The old separate regional/family badges are removed from all redesigned exports. Internal Unicode markers are preserved to avoid namespace drift.

HDR10+ now uses a clean vector reproduction sourced from Samsung artwork, with flat single-color ink across all skins. The former site-header PNG and its baked-in shadow are retained only as a source archive. The SVG attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 license are recorded in the source manifest and `assets/badge-sources/HDR10PLUS-ATTRIBUTION.md`. The other artwork uses monochrome light/dark reverse variants, transparent-margin trimming and proportional resizing. Logo provenance and trademark notices are available on the source pages in the source manifest. Manrope's full license is in `assets/fonts/OFL-Manrope.txt`.

Run `npm run design:badges` to regenerate the glyphs and packs offline. Run `npm run design:preview` for the full PNG contact sheets and interactive local gallery. `npm run test:design` checks export coverage, transparent edges, dimensions, image hashes and logo provenance. `npm test` also verifies the formatter and tier behavior.

`badges/design/gallery.html` shows all four skins and toggles enlarged/native-size views. The 20px previews follow the checked Nuvio TV badge geometry. They are reconstructions, not a device screenshot. The release publishes content-addressed artwork alongside matching formatter and pack files.

Kanso 2.3.0 adds seven matching glyphs: AD audio description, forced captions, SDH, hardcoded HDRip, DVD REMUX, side-by-side 3D and over-under 3D. Accessibility facts use the audio/subtitle category hues and the shared Manrope lettering. Known track flags and titles determine accessibility; no capability is inferred from the logo or the device.
