Design study for Kanso Nuvio streaming metadata CHANNEL badges. One polished UI concept board on dark navy #151A21, no photography. Main design is an elegant compact nearly square rounded outline chip: a simple beautifully balanced speaker outline with two sound waves centered in the UPPER half, the exact number 5.1 below in bold clean Manrope-like sans serif. No side-by-side icon and text. Flat crisp cyan #77DCF1 strokes, no glow, no shadow, no gradients, no extra ornament. Show a large hero 5.1 chip and a tight row of smaller consistent 1.0, 2.0, 2.1, 5.1, 6.1, 7.1, 8.1 chips. Below show matching four skin treatments of same geometry: cyan outline, white glyph transparent background, cyan filled with black glyph, white filled with black glyph. A tiny native size strip should demonstrate readability of the stacked speaker-over-number composition. Restrained premium TV interface aesthetic, intentional spacing, strong numeric counters and decimal points. Title KANSO / CHANNELS. This board is a visual design reference for deterministic production SVG/PNG assets.
